# Executive Sponsor Change & Champion Departure Playbook

> **Purpose:** A rapid 14-day multi-threading and stakeholder stabilization protocol designed to protect renewal continuity when a primary champion, admin, or executive sponsor transitions out of their role.

---

## Stakeholder Transition Risk Assessment

| Departing Role | Immediate Risk Level | Primary Vulnerability | Stabilization Goal |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Executive Sponsor (VP / C-Suite)** | **High / Critical** | Loss of budget ownership, strategic priority shift, or incoming leader bringing a competing vendor | Re-anchor ROI and business outcomes with the incoming executive within 14 days |
| **Day-to-Day Champion / Program Owner** | **Elevated / High** | Operational stall, workflow abandonment, and loss of internal advocacy | Identify and enable the successor power user within 7 business days |
| **Technical / System Admin** | **Moderate / Operational** | Broken integrations, unprovisioned seats, and delayed troubleshooting | Transfer admin permissions, API keys, and runbooks before offboarding |

---

## 14-Day Stakeholder Recovery Protocol

### Days 1–3: Detection, Audit & Internal Triage
* **Confirm Departure & Timeline:** Identify whether the stakeholder is transitioning internally or exiting the company, and verify their final active date.
* **Audit Account Footprint:** Pull active power users, recent support tickets, contract renewal date, and current health score in Salesforce/HubSpot.
* **Flag Renewal Risk:** Update account health status to **Watch / Stakeholder Change** so leadership has visibility into upcoming renewal exposure.

### Days 4–7: Multi-Threading & Successor Identification
* **Request Warm Handover (If Still Active):** Ask the departing champion to introduce their replacement and confirm who owns budget vs. day-to-day execution.
* **Engage Secondary Power Users:** Reach out directly to top active users (by login frequency) to offer operational continuity and uncover informal team leads.
* **Transfer Technical Ownership:** Verify that at least two active admins hold full workspace and billing permissions so access never bottlenecks.

### Days 8–14: Executive Re-Alignment & Value Briefing
* **Deliver a 1-Page Executive Value Brief:** Send the incoming sponsor or department head a concise summary of historical ROI, active adoption metrics, and key milestones achieved.
* **Host a 25-Minute Alignment Call:** Focus on the new leader's priorities for the next two quarters rather than assuming old goals still apply.
* **Update Mutual Success Plan:** Lock in new stakeholder owners, revise quarterly objectives, and schedule the next QBR.
