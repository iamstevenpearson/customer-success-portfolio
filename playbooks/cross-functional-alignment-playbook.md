# Playbook: Cross-Functional Alignment Across Sales, Product, Engineering & Account Management

**Owner:** Senior Customer Success Manager / Strategic Account Manager  
**Portfolio Scope:** Mid Market & Enterprise B2B SaaS ($10M+ ARR / 300+ Accounts)  
**Purpose:** Establish repeatable operating rhythms, clear ownership boundaries, and structured feedback loops across Sales, Account Management, Product, Engineering, and Support to protect retention and accelerate expansion.

---

## 1. Cross-Functional Operating Matrix

| Partner Team | Core Alignment Focus | Cadence & Ritual | Shared Artifact / Output |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Account Executives (Sales)** | Seamless pre-sale to post sale transitions and commercial context transfer | Deal Handoff Call within 48 hours of Closed Won | Standardized Sales to CS Handoff Brief in CRM |
| **Account Managers (AM)** | Joint whitespace mapping, renewal forecasting, and expansion execution | Bi weekly Account Territory Sync & 90 Day Renewal Review | Mutual Growth & Renewal Pipeline Tracker |
| **Product Management** | Connecting Customer Feedback to Revenue Opportunities | Monthly Voice of the Customer (VoC) Product Sync | ARR Weighted Feature Request & Roadmap Brief |
| **Engineering & Support** | Rapid triage of critical bugs, P1 escalations, and technical blockers | Weekly Escalation Triage & Dedicated Incident Channels | Root Cause Analysis (RCA) & Customer Status Update |
| **Implementation & Onboarding** | Eliminating setup bottlenecks and accelerating time to first value | Weekly Kickoff & Go Live Readiness Review | 30 60 90 Day Implementation Scorecard |

---

## 2. Core Cross-Functional Workflows

### A. Sales & Account Management Commercial Alignment
1. **Enforce Handoff Hygiene:** Require completed business goals, technical requirements, stakeholder maps, and risk flags in CRM before scheduling the customer kickoff call.
2. **Define Swimlanes on Expansion:** CSMs surface product adoption milestones, seat saturation, and departmental use cases early; Account Managers co-lead commercial scoping, pricing proposals, and contract redlines.
3. **Run Joint Executive Business Reviews:** Partner with AMs on strategic QBRs so the CSM leads with verified ROI and adoption benchmarks while the AM presents multi-year growth options.

### B. Product & Engineering Voice of the Customer Loop
1. **Quantify Every Feature Request:** Log customer feedback in CRM or Jira tied directly to account ARR, renewal date, and business impact rather than passing along isolated one-off anecdotes.
2. **Close the Loop With Customers:** Maintain a release tracker so CSMs immediately notify requesting champions when a feature enters beta or general availability, turning product updates into adoption wins.
3. ** De-escalate Technical Incidents Calmly:** Translate engineering diagnostics into plain business language for customer executives while shielding engineers from ad hoc status pings during active incident resolution.

---

## 3. Escalation & Governance Guardrails

* **Single Source of Truth:** Document all handoff notes, escalation owners, product tickets, and renewal commitments inside CRM (Salesforce, HubSpot, Gainsight, or Vitally) so every team operates from identical account context.
* **No Surprises Forecasting:** Flag any technical blocker or product gap threatening a renewal at least 90 days out so Product, Engineering, and AM leadership can intervene proactively.
