# Playbook: Core CSM & Account Management KPIs and Execution Framework

**Owner:** Senior Customer Success Manager / Strategic Account Manager  
**Portfolio Scope:** Mid Market & Enterprise B2B SaaS ($10M+ ARR / 300+ Accounts)  
**Purpose:** Define the core retention, adoption, and revenue metrics that govern Customer Success and Account Management, paired with repeatable operational workflows to hit and exceed targets every quarter.

---

## 1. Core KPI Scorecard & Benchmarks

| Metric | Definition & Formula | Target Benchmark | Primary Leading Indicators |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Gross Revenue Retention (GRR)** | `(Starting ARR minus Contraction minus Churn) / Starting ARR` | **92% to 95%+** | Onboarding velocity, weekly active license utilization, executive sponsor engagement |
| **Net Revenue Retention (NRR)** | `(Starting ARR + Expansion minus Contraction minus Churn) / Starting ARR` | **105% to 115%+** | Seat capacity thresholds, multi-product adoption, QBR business case alignment |
| **Product Adoption & Seat Utilization** | `Active Weekly Users / Total Licensed Seats` (plus core workflow completion) | **80%+ active seats** | Time to First Value (TTFV), workflow automation setup, power user retention |
| **Time to First Value (TTFV)** | Days from contract signature to first verified customer business outcome | **Under 21 days** (SMB/Mid Market) / **Under 45 days** (Enterprise) | Clean Sales to CS handoff, kickoff completion within 5 business days |
| **Customer Health Score** | Weighted composite of product telemetry, support ticket trends, and relationship sentiment | **85%+ Green Accounts** | Login frequency, feature depth, Executive Business Review attendance |
| **QBR / EBR Cadence Coverage** | Percentage of strategic and renewal-eligible accounts completing a structured business review | **90%+ every 90 days** | Documented ROI metrics, mutual success plan updates, multi-threaded stakeholders |

---

## 2. How to Approach & Hit Each KPI

### Gross Revenue Retention (GRR) & Churn Prevention
* **Start Renewal Defense 120 Days Out:** Never treat renewal as a 30-day administrative task. Audit seat utilization, support escalations, and champion stability at T minus 120, 90, and 60 days inside Salesforce, HubSpot, Gainsight, or Vitally.
* **Run 30 Day Stabilization Sprints on Yellow/Red Accounts:** When usage drops or a champion leaves, immediately diagnose the workflow bottleneck, deliver targeted async enablement (Scribe + Loom), and lock in weekly milestone check ins until health returns to Green.
* ** Multi-Thread Every Strategic Account:** Build relationships across at least three layers (day-to-day power users, operational managers, and the economic buyer) so single point turnover never threatens a renewal.

### Net Revenue Retention (NRR) & Expansion
* **Lead With Verified Value Before Commercial Asks:** Anchor every expansion conversation in documented Year 1 outcomes such as hours saved, manual workflows eliminated, or revenue influenced.
* **Trigger Discovery at 80% Seat Capacity:** Use automated telemetry alerts when an account crosses 80% license utilization or hits tier limits to initiate consultative growth conversations ahead of upcoming team launches or seasonal surges.
* **Build CFO Ready Business Cases:** Frame add-on tiers, extra seats, and multi-year co terms around lower per-unit economics and measurable payback periods rather than feature lists.

### Product Adoption & Time to First Value (TTFV)
* **Standardize the Sales to CS Handoff:** Capture the customer's top three business goals, technical requirements, and key stakeholders before kickoff so the first call focuses on configuration rather than repeating discovery.
* **Eliminate Spreadsheet Workarounds Early:** Identify manual legacy habits during onboarding and configure native platform workflows (approvals, unified inboxes, automated reporting) within the first 14 days.
* **Scale Enablement With Reusable Assets:** Pair live kickoff sessions with role-specific Scribe walkthroughs and 3-minute Loom videos so new hires onboard smoothly without stalling team adoption.

### Executive Business Reviews (QBRs / EBRs)
* **Shift From Status Updates to Strategic Alignment:** Spend no more than 20% of a QBR reviewing historical usage metrics; dedicate 80% to business impact, upcoming company initiatives, and a joint roadmap for the next 90 days.
* **Deliver 1 Page Executive Readouts:** Summarize ROI, health status, risks, and growth recommendations on a single scannable page that champions can forward directly to their CFO or VP.

---

## 3. Operating Rhythm for Quota & KPI Attainment

* **Weekly Pipeline & Risk Triage (Monday):** Review Red/Yellow health alerts, open renewal forecasts within 90 days, and accounts crossing 80% seat capacity.
* **Mid Quarter Forecast Calibration (Week 6):** Audit every renewal and expansion opportunity in CRM with concrete next steps, verified economic buyer sign off, and updated close dates.
* **Post QBR Action Tracking (Within 24 Hours):** Send the 1 page QBR summary with assigned owners, target dates, and mutual accountability milestones logged in CRM.
