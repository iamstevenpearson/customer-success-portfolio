# Expansion & Upsell Discovery Playbook

> **Purpose:** A consultative, value-led commercial growth framework designed to identify natural seat, feature-tier, and cross-departmental expansion triggers and convert proven adoption into Net Revenue Retention (NRR) growth.

---

## Commercial Expansion Trigger Matrix

| Expansion Vector | Telemetry / Account Trigger | Qualification Signal | Commercial Motion |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Seat & License Expansion** | Active seat utilization exceeds **90%** for 30+ days OR waitlist requests arrive from admins | Hiring growth, new team onboarding, or seasonal volume surge | Co-termed add-on order form or mid-term true-up |
| **Feature / Tier Upgrade** | Repeated usage of capped features, API rate-limit warnings, or security/SSO audit requests | Need for enterprise governance, automation, or advanced analytics | Tier upgrade positioned around time saved & risk reduction |
| **Cross-Department Rollout** | Organic inbound inquiries from adjacent business units (e.g., Ops, Finance, Sales) | Proven ROI in initial department creating internal executive demand | Multi-lane pilot or enterprise site-license expansion |

---

## Consultative Expansion Workflow

1. **Validate Value First (Health Gate):** Never pitch expansion into an unresolved `SEV-1/SEV-2` issue or a Red health score; confirm core KPIs are green before opening commercial scope.
2. **Quantify Current ROI:** Package hard usage metrics and time/cost savings into a crisp QBR or executive checkpoint readout.
3. **Run Gap & Friction Discovery:** Ask forward-looking workflow questions (*"Where is your team still relying on manual spreadsheets outside the platform?"* or *"Which adjacent teams depend on these outputs?"*).
4. **Partner with Account Management / AE:** Align on pricing structure, co-terming options, and executive proposal framing so the customer experiences one unified commercial team.
5. **Tie Expansion to Onboarding Milestones:** Once closed, immediately trigger a mini-onboarding sprint for new seats or modules so expansion revenue sticks through renewal.

---

## QBR Expansion Discovery Prompts

* *"You're currently at 94% seat capacity—how is team headcount scaling over the next two quarters?"*
* *"Which workflows are still creating manual handoffs between your team and [Adjacent Department]?"*
* *"If we automated [Specific Bottleneck], what would that free your leads up to focus on?"*
