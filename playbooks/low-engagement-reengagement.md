# Low Engagement & Silent Churn Re-Engagement Playbook

> **Purpose:** A proactive intervention framework designed to identify "silent churn" signals early, diagnose root causes behind declining product utilization, and re-engage stalled accounts before renewal risk becomes critical.

---

## Silent Churn Early-Warning Signals

| Signal Category | Quantitative / Behavioral Trigger | Risk Tier | Diagnostic Focus |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Login & Seat Decay** | Weekly active users (WAU) drop **≥ 25%** over 30 days, or **< 50%** of purchased seats active | **Yellow / Watch** | License bloat vs. onboarding drop-off |
| **Feature Depth Stall** | Account uses only basic login/viewing features while ignoring core sticky workflows | **Yellow / Watch** | Training gap or missing integration |
| **Stakeholder Ghosting** | Champion misses two consecutive check-ins or stops responding to async updates for 30+ days | **Orange / Elevated** | Role change, competing priority, or silent dissatisfaction |
| **Support & Admin Silence** | Zero support tickets, zero admin logins, and zero configuration updates over 60 days | **Orange / Elevated** | Shelfware risk prior to renewal runway |

---

## 3-Step Re-Engagement Protocol (Days 1–21)

### Step 1: Pre-Outreach Telemetry Audit (Days 1–3)
* **Pull Usage Cohorts:** Identify which specific teams or power users dropped off vs. which workflows remain active.
* **Check Recent Friction:** Review Zendesk/support history for unresolved bugs, friction points, or feature gaps that may have caused users to abandon the tool.
* **Verify Stakeholder Roster:** Confirm on LinkedIn and in CRM whether the primary champion or executive sponsor changed roles.

### Step 2: Value-First Pattern Interrupt (Days 4–10)
* **Avoid the "Just Checking In" Email:** Lead with a specific telemetry insight or benchmark paired with a concrete quick win.
* **Multi-Thread Outreach:** If the primary champion is unresponsive after two attempts, engage secondary power users or re-anchor with the Executive Sponsor around original business outcomes.
* **Deliver an Async Micro-Audit:** Share a 2-minute Loom video or 3-bullet snapshot highlighting underutilized features tied directly to their team's KPIs.

### Step 3: Re-Calibration & Enablement Sprint (Days 11–21)
* **Run a 20-Minute Reset Session:** Re-align on current team priorities—discarding irrelevant setup steps and focusing on one high-impact workflow.
* **Targeted Refresher Training:** Host a focused 30-minute workshop for inactive user groups with updated SOP templates.
* **30-Day Adoption Checkpoint:** Set a mutual milestone target (e.g., lifting active seat usage back above 75%) and log progress in the account health scorecard.
