# Playbook: Customer Health Scoring & Retention Framework

**Owner:** Senior Customer Success Manager / Strategic Account Manager  
**Portfolio Scope:** Mid Market & Enterprise B2B SaaS ($8M+ ARR)  
**Purpose:** Standardize proactive account health tracking, early churn risk detection, and outcome-driven customer cadences to protect Gross Revenue Retention (GRR) and drive predictable renewals.

---

## 1. Weighted Customer Health Score Model (100-Point Scale)

Every account is evaluated continuously across four weighted pillars inside CRM and CS tooling (Salesforce, HubSpot, Gainsight, or Vitally):

| Customer Health Category | Weight | Leading Indicators Tracked | Red Flag Threshold |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Product Adoption & Depth** | 35% | Weekly active users (WAU), license seat utilization, and core workflow completion | Seat utilization below 60% or 20%+ drop in weekly active usage over 14 days |
| **Executive & Champion Engagement** | 25% | Cadence attendance, QBR participation, and broad stakeholder alignment | Primary champion unresponsive for 21 days or sole champion dependency |
| **Business Outcome Realization** | 25% | Documented ROI milestones, Mutual Success Plan progress, and benchmark gains | Unclear success criteria at Day 60 or stalled progress against core KPIs |
| **Support & Sentiment Health** | 15% | Open P1/P2 escalations, time to resolution, CSAT/NPS feedback, and billing status | Unresolved critical bug open 7+ days or detracting NPS/CSAT response |

---

## 2. Health Levels & Action Plans

| Score Range | Health Status | Engagement Cadence | Required Operational Action |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **80 to 100** | **Healthy (Green)** | Monthly strategic check-in & Quarterly Business Review (QBR) | Capture ROI case study metrics, map departmental whitespace, and introduce expansion options |
| **60 to 79** | **Watchlist (Yellow)** | Biweekly adoption check-in & targeted workflow enablement | Audit underutilized features, assign custom Scribe/Loom guides, and re-engage secondary stakeholders |
| **0 to 59** | **At Risk (Red)** | Weekly executive stabilization call & internal escalation sync | Launch a 30 Day Get Well Plan, loop in Executive Sponsor and Product/Engineering, and log recovery milestones |

---

## 3. Outcome Driven Renewal Runway (180 Day Countdown)

1. **T Minus 180 Days (Value Audit & Stakeholder Mapping):** Audit 6-month product telemetry, confirm executive sponsor alignment, and identify any unaddressed adoption gaps before renewal pricing is discussed.
2. **T Minus 120 Days (Executive Business Review):** Deliver a 1 Page Executive Value Summary tying verified platform usage directly to customer revenue, efficiency, or cost savings goals.
3. **T Minus 90 Days (Commercial Alignment & Forecast Lock):** Partner with Account Management to confirm contract terms, seat adjustments, or multi-year growth options, locking an accurate renewal forecast in CRM.
4. **T Minus 30 Days (Signature & Next Year Success Plan):** Finalize paperwork early and transition directly into the next annual Mutual Success Plan so momentum carries seamlessly past the renewal date.

---

## 4. Operational Governance

* **Weekly Portfolio Triage:** Review all Yellow and Red accounts every Monday morning, verifying that every at-risk account has a documented owner, root cause tag, and next action date in CRM.
* **Leading Over Lagging Indicators:** Intervene on engagement dips and champion changes 90+ days ahead of contract expiration rather than waiting for renewal notice windows.
