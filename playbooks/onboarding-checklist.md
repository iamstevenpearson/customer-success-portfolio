# 30-60-90 Day Onboarding Blueprint (SMB to Enterprise)

> **Purpose:** A repeatable, right-sized implementation framework designed to compress time-to-value, standardize Sales-to-CS handoffs, and establish early executive alignment across high-velocity SMB teams and multi-lane enterprise accounts.

---

## Segment Calibration Matrix

| Segment | Kickoff Window | Cadence & Governance | Primary Success Criteria |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **SMB / Commercial** | Within 3 business days | Async digital enablement + 2 live milestone checkpoints | Core workflow live in <14 days; 80%+ active seat utilization |
| **Mid-Market** | Within 5 business days | Weekly implementation calls + Day 30 sponsor pulse | Primary integration validated; repeatable team playbooks active |
| **Enterprise** | Within 5 business days | Formal project plan, steering committee & executive sponsor sync | Multi-department rollout, SSO/API production sign-off & ROI baseline |

---

## Phase 1: Days 1–30 — Foundation, Handoff & First Value

* **Sales-to-CS Handoff (T-48 Hours):** Review CRM deal notes, commercial terms, promised outcomes, technical requirements, and key stakeholders before customer kickoff.
* **Executive Kickoff Call:** Confirm business objectives, define measurable success criteria (owner, target metric, deadline), and lock in mutual implementation dates.
* **Technical & Data Readiness:** Configure workspace permissions, validate sandbox-to-production integrations, and verify clean data ingestion.
* **Role-Based Enablement:** Deliver separate admin configuration sessions and end-user workflow training tailored to daily job functions.
* **Day 30 Sponsor Check-In:** Validate initial adoption signals, surface early friction points, and confirm readiness to transition out of technical setup.

---

## Phase 2: Days 31–60 — Workflow Adoption & Habit Formation

* **Telemetry & Usage Audit:** Review active user rates, feature depth, and automated health-score alerts to spot under-adopted teams early.
* **Champion Enablement:** Equip internal power users with quick-reference guides and internal SOP templates so usage scales organically.
* **Process Standardization:** Embed platform workflows into the customer's existing weekly operations rather than treating the tool as an isolated login.
* **Mid-Point Risk Check:** Resolve open support escalations and close training gaps before early momentum stalls.

---

## Phase 3: Days 61–90 — Value Realization & Executive Alignment

* **90-Day Value & ROI Readout:** Quantify early operational wins (time saved, workflow completion lift, adoption velocity) against Day 1 baseline goals.
* **Graduation to Steady-State CS:** Transition the account from implementation cadence into ongoing lifecycle management and health-score monitoring.
* **First QBR Scheduled:** Lock in the inaugural Quarterly Business Review with the executive sponsor and identify adjacent team expansion opportunities.
