# Playbook: AI & Automated Workflow Systems for Customer Success

**Owner:** Senior Customer Success Manager / Strategic Account Manager  
**Portfolio Scope:** Mid Market & Enterprise B2B SaaS ($10M+ ARR / 300+ Accounts)  
**Purpose:** Eliminate repetitive administrative overhead and scale proactive account management across large portfolios using AI-assisted synthesis, automated telemetry triggers, and CRM lifecycle workflows.

---

## 1. Automation Architecture & Use Cases

| Workflow Area | Trigger Event | Automated Action | Strategic Impact |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Early Churn Risk Alerts** | Weekly active usage drops 20%+ or key champion goes inactive for 14 days | Auto creates a high-priority CRM task, alerts the CSM, and stages a value-first re-engagement email draft | Catches stalled adoption weeks before renewal risk escalates |
| **Expansion Signal Detection** | Seat utilization crosses 80% or account hits plan tier limits | Flags account as Expansion Ready in CRM and triggers a consultative discovery checklist | Turns organic usage growth into predictable NRR expansion |
| ** AI-Assisted QBR Preparation** | 14 days before a scheduled Quarterly Business Review | Synthesizes 90-day product telemetry, support ticket themes, and call transcripts into a 1-page executive brief | Cuts QBR prep time by 70% while elevating strategic insight |
| **Automated Onboarding Milestones** | Contract signed or kickoff call completed | Launches role-based Scribe and Loom enablement sequences and tracks time to first value checkpoints | Accelerates onboarding consistency across SMB, mid-market, and enterprise tiers |
| **Call Transcript & CRM Hygiene** | Customer discovery, QBR, or renewal call concludes | Extracts action items, stakeholder sentiment, renewal blockers, and next steps directly into CRM fields | Ensures clean forecast visibility without manual post-call data entry |

---

## 2. Automation Boundaries: Where Human Judgment Matters Most

1. **Automate Preparation, Never Executive Relationships:** Use AI to summarize call notes, spot usage anomalies, and draft initial QBR readouts, while keeping strategic framing, renewal negotiations, and executive alignment 100% human-led.
2. **Verify Data Before Sharing Externally:** Always audit AI-generated usage summaries and ROI calculations against source CRM and product telemetry before presenting to customer stakeholders.
3. **Protect Customer Privacy & Confidentiality:** Never input sensitive customer PII, proprietary financial records, or confidential contract terms into unapproved public AI tools.

---

## 3. Implementation

* **Single Source of Truth:** Sync all automated health alerts, lifecycle stages, and expansion flags directly into your core CRM and Customer Success platform (Salesforce, HubSpot, Gainsight, or Vitally).
* **Quarterly Workflow Audit:** Review automated email sequences and alert thresholds every quarter to eliminate alert fatigue and keep messaging aligned with current product releases.
