# Playbook: AI & Automated Workflow Systems for Customer Success

**Owner:** Senior Customer Success Manager / Strategic Account Manager  
**Portfolio Scope:** Mid-Market & Enterprise B2B SaaS  
**Purpose:** Eliminate repetitive administrative overhead and scale proactive account management across large customer portfolios using AI-assisted synthesis, automated telemetry triggers, and CRM lifecycle workflows.

This playbook outlines a practical framework for incorporating AI and automation into Customer Success operations. The objective is to give CSMs more time to focus on strategic customer engagement, retention, adoption, and revenue growth while improving operational consistency and portfolio visibility.

---

## 1. Automation Architecture & Use Cases

| Workflow Area | Trigger Event | Automated Action | Strategic Impact |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Early Churn Risk Alerts** | Weekly active usage drops 20%+ or key champion becomes inactive for 14 days | Creates a high-priority CRM task, alerts the CSM, and prepares a value-first re-engagement email draft | Identifies declining adoption before renewal risk escalates |
| **Expansion Signal Detection** | Seat utilization crosses 80% or account approaches plan limits | Flags account as Expansion Ready in CRM and generates a consultative discovery checklist | Converts organic usage growth into qualified expansion opportunities |
| **AI-Assisted QBR Preparation** | 14 days before a scheduled Quarterly Business Review | Synthesizes 90-day product telemetry, support ticket themes, and call transcripts into a one-page executive brief | Reduces preparation time while improving strategic account visibility |
| **Automated Onboarding Milestones** | Contract signed or kickoff call completed | Launches role-based Scribe and Loom enablement sequences and tracks time-to-first-value milestones | Improves onboarding consistency and accelerates product adoption |
| **Call Transcript & CRM Hygiene** | Customer discovery, QBR, or renewal call concludes | Extracts action items, stakeholder sentiment, renewal blockers, and next steps for CRM review | Improves documentation and forecast visibility while reducing administrative work |

*Note: Trigger thresholds are illustrative starting points and should be calibrated to product usage patterns, customer segments, and historical account performance.*

## 2. Practical Workflow: AI-Assisted Churn Prevention

**Scenario:** A mid-market customer has experienced declining product adoption over the past 30 days. Their primary champion has also stopped attending recurring check-ins, and their renewal is approaching within 90 days.

### Step 1: Identify the Risk

Product telemetry identifies:
- A 25% decline in weekly active usage.
- Reduced engagement from previously active users.
- No recent activity from the primary customer champion.

The customer health score decreases, triggering an automated CRM alert.

### Step 2: Diagnose the Situation

AI consolidates available account information, including:
- Product adoption trends.
- Recent support tickets.
- Previous meeting notes and call transcripts.
- Open customer requests.
- Renewal timeline and stakeholder engagement.

The system generates a concise account risk summary highlighting potential adoption and relationship concerns.

### Step 3: Prioritize and Engage

The CSM reviews the information, validates the underlying data, and determines the appropriate next steps.

Potential actions include:
- Reconnecting with the primary champion.
- Identifying additional stakeholders.
- Scheduling an adoption review.
- Offering targeted product training.
- Escalating unresolved support concerns.

AI can assist with preparing personalized outreach, but the CSM owns the customer relationship and final communication.

### Step 4: Execute a Recovery Plan

The CSM establishes a 30-day recovery plan with defined milestones.

| Timeline | Action | Desired Outcome |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Days 1–5 | Re-engage champion and identify adoption barriers | Understand root cause |
| Days 6–10 | Deliver targeted training and resolve blockers | Restore product engagement |
| Days 11–20 | Monitor usage and conduct stakeholder follow-up | Measure adoption recovery |
| Days 21–30 | Review progress and establish ongoing success milestones | Stabilize account health |

### Step 5: Measure Results

The CSM evaluates:
- Weekly active user trends.
- Feature adoption.
- Stakeholder engagement.
- Support ticket resolution.
- Progress against customer success objectives.
- Renewal confidence.

The goal is not simply to improve a health score. It is to restore customer value, strengthen the relationship, and reduce renewal risk.

## 3. AI-Assisted QBR Preparation

Quarterly Business Reviews are an opportunity to demonstrate business value, align stakeholders, and identify opportunities for continued growth.

AI can significantly reduce preparation time by consolidating information that would otherwise require manual research across multiple systems.

### Information Sources

- CRM account history and renewal information.
- Product usage and adoption dashboards.
- Customer support trends and unresolved tickets.
- Previous QBR notes and success plans.
- Call transcripts and stakeholder feedback.

### Generated Executive Brief

The AI-assisted workflow prepares a structured summary containing:

1. **Account Overview:** Customer objectives, contract status, and key stakeholders.
2. **Adoption Performance:** Usage trends, feature engagement, and adoption gaps.
3. **Customer Health:** Current risks, support themes, and relationship sentiment.
4. **Business Outcomes:** Progress against established success metrics.
5. **Growth Opportunities:** Potential expansion signals and additional use cases.
6. **Next Steps:** Recommended actions, owners, and timelines.

The CSM reviews and validates the brief before using it in customer-facing discussions.

**Operating Principle:** Data tells us where to look. Customer conversations tell us why.

## 4. Automation Architecture & Technology Stack

An effective automation strategy connects customer data, CRM workflows, and AI-assisted analysis.

| Technology | Operational Role |
| :--- | :--- |
| **HubSpot / Salesforce** | CRM records, account segmentation, lifecycle management, and automated task creation |
| **Vitally / Gainsight** | Customer health scoring, adoption monitoring, and portfolio management |
| **Looker** | Product usage analytics, reporting, and performance visibility |
| **AI / LLM Tools** | Call synthesis, account summaries, risk analysis, and personalized communication drafts |
| **Zapier / Make** | Workflow orchestration and connections between supported applications |
| **Loom / Scribe** | Customer enablement, instructional content, and repeatable onboarding resources |

*Technology selection depends on the organization's existing infrastructure, integrations, security requirements, and operational needs.*
