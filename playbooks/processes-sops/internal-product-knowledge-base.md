# Internal Product Knowledge & Enablement System

> **Purpose:** A centralized internal knowledge base and enablement workflow designed to keep Customer Success, Support, and Account Management teams aligned on product capabilities, release changes, technical edge cases, and repeatable customer solutions.

---

## Core Knowledge Architecture

| Knowledge Pillar | Contents & Artifacts | Primary Owner | Update Cadence |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Product & Feature Deep Dives** | Core workflow guides, configuration prerequisites, permissions matrices, and known limitations | CS Operations & Product | On every feature release |
| **Release Enablement Briefs** | Internal "What Changed & Why It Matters" summaries, customer talk tracks, and migration steps | Customer Success Lead | Bi-weekly / Sprint release |
| **Troubleshooting & Edge Cases** | Root-cause runbooks, workaround steps, log-reading guides, and escalation criteria | Senior CSM & Tier-2 Support | Weekly / Post-incident |
| **Industry & Use-Case Playbooks** | Vertical-specific configurations (SMB, Mid-Market, Enterprise) and ROI proof points | Customer Success Team | Quarterly |

---

## Closed-Loop Knowledge Capture Workflow

1. **Trigger Identification:** Flag recurring support tickets, onboarding friction points, or new feature releases requiring team-wide documentation.
2. **Drafting & Standardization:** Capture the issue, resolution steps, screenshots/loom walkthroughs, and customer-safe messaging in a standardized Markdown template.
3. **Peer & Technical Validation:** Review technical accuracy with Support or Product Engineering before publishing to prevent outdated workarounds.
4. **Team Enablement & Rollout:** Share updates in weekly CS standups and link articles directly inside CRM/ticketing workflows (HubSpot, Salesforce, Zendesk) for instant access during live calls.
5. **Quarterly Audit & Pruning:** Archive deprecated workflows and verify high-traffic guides against current UI and API behavior.

---

## Measurable Team Impact

* **Faster Ramp Time:** Shortens new-hire CSM and support onboarding with self-serve product documentation.
* **Reduced Escalation Volume:** Equips frontline CSMs to resolve configuration and workflow questions without engineering handoffs.
* **Consistent Customer Messaging:** Ensures every CSM communicates product capabilities, workarounds, and roadmap updates accurately.
