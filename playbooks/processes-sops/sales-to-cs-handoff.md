# Sales-to-CS Handoff Standard Operating Procedure (SOP)

> **Purpose:** A structured post-sale transition protocol designed to prevent context loss, eliminate repeated discovery questions, and accelerate time-to-value across SMB, mid-market, and enterprise accounts.

---

## Handoff Timeline & Ownership

| Milestone | Timing | Primary Owner | Deliverable / Gate |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Deal Stage: Commit / Closed-Won** | T-0 (Signature) | Account Executive (AE) | Automated CRM notification + Handoff Intake Form completed |
| **Internal Handoff Sync** | Within 24–48 business hours | AE + Assigned CSM | 20-minute internal alignment call + stakeholder mapping |
| **Customer Warm Introduction** | Within 48 business hours | AE (intro) → CSM (owner) | Executive intro email + kickoff scheduling link sent |
| **Kickoff Call Execution** | Within 3–5 business days | CSM | Mutual Success Plan baseline + technical prerequisites locked |

---

## Mandatory CRM Intake Checklist (AE Gate)

Before the internal handoff call occurs, the Account Executive documents the following fields in Salesforce / HubSpot:

* **Commercial & Contract Terms:** ARR/ACV, license tier, seat count, billing cadence, contract start/renewal dates, and any non-standard SLA or opt-out clauses.
* **Business Drivers & Pain Points:** The core problems that triggered the purchase and the specific metrics the buyer needs to move.
* **Promised Outcomes & Scope:** Explicit deliverables, custom integrations, or timeline commitments made during the sales cycle.
* **Stakeholder & Buying Committee Map:** Economic Buyer (EB), Executive Sponsor, Day-to-Day Champion, Technical/IT Lead, and Billing Contact.
* **Known Risks & Red Flags:** Competing internal priorities, tight go-live deadlines, change-management resistance, or unresolved technical questions.

---

## Internal Handoff Agenda (20 Minutes)

1. **Why They Bought (5 mins):** AE walks through the customer's business context, urgency, and desired ROI—not just the feature list.
2. **Who's Who & Political Dynamics (5 mins):** Confirm executive sponsor engagement level and identify internal champions vs. skeptics.
3. **Technical & Scope Validation (5 mins):** Verify data sources, SSO/API dependencies, and whether implementation requires Solutions Engineering support.
4. **Kickoff Strategy & Framing (5 mins):** Align on the opening narrative for the customer kickoff so the client feels momentum from day one.
