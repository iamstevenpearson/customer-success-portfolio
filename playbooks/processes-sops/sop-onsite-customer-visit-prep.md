# SOP: Preparing & Executing High-Impact Onsite Customer Visits

**Owner:** Senior Customer Success Manager / Strategic Account Manager  
**Audience:** Customer Success, Account Management, Solutions Engineering, Executive Sponsors  
**Purpose:** Maximize the strategic return of in-person customer visits by aligning pre-visit intelligence, multi-threaded stakeholder agendas, live workflow audits, and concrete post-visit accountability.

---

## 1. Visit Objectives & Success Criteria

Every onsite visit should anchor around one primary commercial or operational goal before travel is booked:

| Visit Type | Primary Objective | Key Deliverable |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Executive Alignment & Renewal** | Reinforce Year 1 ROI, align with C-suite priorities, and de-risk upcoming renewal | 1 Page Executive Value Summary & Mutual Success Plan |
| **Account Recovery & Workflow Audit** | Diagnose adoption challenges on the ground with end users and rebuild momentum | 30 Day Stabilization Roadmap & Live Deskside Enablement |
| **Expansion & Multi-Team Rollout** | Scope new departments, locations, or product tiers ahead of a multi-year growth deal | Expansion Readiness Assessment & CFO Business Case |

---

## 2. Pre-Visit Preparation Timeline

### T Minus 14 Days: Account Intelligence & Stakeholder Mapping
1. **Audit CRM & Product Telemetry:** Pull 90-day usage trends, active license utilization, open support escalations, and renewal dates from Salesforce, HubSpot, Gainsight, or Vitally.
2. **Map the Room:** Identify every confirmed attendee by role (economic buyer, operational champion, day-to-day power users) and note recent organizational changes or new hires.
3. **Align Internal Teams:** Brief Solutions Engineering, Product, or Executive Sponsors on account history, sensitive friction points, and specific questions to listen for.

### T Minus 7 Days: Co-Create the Agenda & Pre-Read
1. **Send a Collaborative Agenda:** Share a time-boxed schedule with the primary champion at least one week prior, reserving 70% of the session for customer discussion and workflow observation rather than slide presentations.
2. **Distribute a 1 Page Executive Pre-Read:** Send a concise snapshot of verified usage wins, key metrics, and three strategic discussion prompts so leadership walks in aligned.
3. **Confirm Logistics:** Verify building security check-in requirements, AV/screen sharing setup, Wi Fi access, and catering or working lunch details.

### T Minus 2 Days: Materials & Demo Readiness
1. **Prepare Offline Backups:** Export your 1-page QBR readout and deck to PDF on your laptop in case guest Wi Fi is restricted.
2. **Stage a Tailored Demo Environment:** Preload workflows, dashboards, or new features relevant to the customer's exact industry and upcoming initiatives.

---

## 3. Onsite Execution Playbook

1. **Open With Executive Gratitude & Outcomes (First 10 Minutes):** Thank the team for hosting, restate their core business goals, and confirm the top two outcomes everyone wants to walk away with.
2. **Observe Real End User Workflows (Shadowing):** Sit beside coordinators or power users to watch how they actually use the platform in real time, noting any spreadsheet workarounds or friction points that never surface on Zoom calls.
3. **Facilitate Strategic Roadmap Alignment:** Connect product capabilities to the company's next two quarters (new locations, product launches, seasonal peaks) and document expansion triggers.
4. **Close With Verbal Action Confirmation (Final 10 Minutes):** Summarize agreed next steps, assign an owner and target date to every item, and confirm the follow-up check-in date before leaving the room.

---

## 4. Post-Visit Follow-Up (Within 24 Hours)

* **Send the Executive Recap Email:** Deliver a crisp summary of key takeaways, agreed milestones, owners, and attached 1-page readouts within 24 hours while momentum is high.
* **Log Visit Intelligence in CRM:** Record stakeholder sentiment, newly uncovered expansion opportunities, and product feedback in CRM and share relevant insights with Product and Sales leadership.
* **Deliver Promised Enablement:** Send any custom Scribe guides, Loom walkthroughs, or configuration updates committed during the onsite within 48 hours.
