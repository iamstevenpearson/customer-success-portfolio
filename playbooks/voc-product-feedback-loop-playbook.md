# Playbook: Voice of the Customer (VoC) & Product Feedback Loop

**Owner:** Senior Customer Success Manager / Strategic Account Manager  
**Portfolio Scope:** Mid Market & Enterprise B2B SaaS ($10M+ ARR / 300+ Accounts)  
**Purpose:** Transform unstructured customer requests from QBRs, support tickets, and executive calls into revenue-weighted product intelligence that informs roadmap prioritization and strengthens customer retention.

---

## 1. End-to-End Feedback Lifecycle

| Stage | Cadence | Owner | Action & Output |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **1. Capture & Tag** | Continuous (Within 24 hours of call) | Customer Success & Support | Log every feature gap, workflow friction point, or enhancement request in CRM and Jira with standardized category tags |
| **2. Quantify Business Impact** | Weekly | Customer Success Manager | Attach account ARR, renewal date, expansion dollars at stake, and workaround severity to each logged request |
| **3. Synthesize & Prioritize** | Monthly | CS Ops & Product Management | Group recurring themes across accounts by total ARR impact and strategic segment during the Monthly VoC Review |
| **4. Beta & Co Design** | Quarterly | Product Management & CSM | Recruit high engagement champions to test early prototypes, validate UX workflows, and provide pre-release feedback |
| **5. Close the Loop** | Within 48 hours of release | Customer Success Manager | Notify every requesting stakeholder when their requested feature ships, turning roadmap delivery into a retention and adoption win |

---

## 2. Standardized Feedback Intake Rubric

To keep product feedback actionable rather than anecdotal, every entry logged into CRM or Jira must include four data points:

1. **Core Problem Statement:** Describe the exact business workflow the customer is trying to accomplish rather than prescribing a specific UI button or technical build.
2. **Commercial Weight:** Record current account ARR, upcoming renewal window, and whether the gap blocks an active expansion or creates churn risk.
3. **Frequency & Affected Personas:** Specify whether the friction impacts daily power users, team admins, or executive reporting stakeholders.
4. **Current Workaround:** Note whether the team relies on manual spreadsheet exports, third-party tools, or custom scripts to bridge the gap today.

---

## 3. Monthly Product & CS Alignment Ritual

* **Top 5 Customer Priorities by Revenue Impact:** Highlight the five biggest customer pain points, considering how much ARR is affected and how many accounts have raised the same concern.
* **Win/Loss & Churn Feedback:** Share common themes from recent renewals, lost deals, and customer departures so Product understands where usability issues or missing features may be affecting retention.
* **Roadmap Communication:** Give CSMs clear, approved language around what's planned, what's still being explored, and what's not currently on the roadmap so they can set realistic expectations with customers.

---

## 4. Closing the Loop With Customers

1. **Maintain a Requestor Watchlist:** Track every customer contact linked to an open product ticket inside CRM (Salesforce, HubSpot, Gainsight, or Vitally).
2. **Deliver Proactive Release Wins:** When a requested enhancement reaches beta or general availability, send a personalized message with a concise Loom walkthrough or Scribe guide showing the feature live in their workflow.
3. **Highlight Shipped Feedback in QBRs:** Dedicate one section of every Quarterly Business Review to showing customers how their direct feedback shaped recent product updates.
